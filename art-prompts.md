# Painting prompts — one per verse

Save each finished painting in `src/art/` named by its number (`00.jpg`, `01.jpg` … `42.jpg`).
The site picks it up automatically. Square images with the figure in the centre work best on
both phones and desktops.

**Add this style line to the end of every prompt:**

> Reverent devotional oil painting in the style of classical Indian temple painting. Very dark umber and black background, lit only by warm gold and saffron light. Main subject centred in the frame. Rich, painterly, dignified, no text, no watermark.
